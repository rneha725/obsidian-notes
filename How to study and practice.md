### How to study
---
##### Practices and tools
- If it is not challenging enough, you are not learning.
- Active Recall
	- Pick and random topic and write
	- Practice after a study session or after some days: scheduling active recall
- Interleaving:
	- In same study sessions, mix up the topics.
- Spaced Repetition: schedule active recall.
- Environment Matters:
	- Try studying in varied environments or mind state to improve active recall
	- Or study in same environment where you will be tested.
- Practice and Quizzing
	- Make is more and more challenging, prediction error => makes stronger pathways.
- Progressively Challenge
	- Assess challenge, find gaps
- Interrogation:
	- Using whys -> chained
	- Explain things in simple language.
